Block 1 of the Microsoft's Digital-Work Platform deep dive - context and scope.

Time: 45 minutes · Goal: What the platform is, where its boundary sits, and why it exists

Focus from mind map

  • Use synced/indexed content when discovery, search quality, and repeated knowledge retrieval matter more than instantaneous freshness.
  • Metadata, such as title, URL, content type, author, timestamps, tags, and business identifiers
  • An access control list that maps source-system entitlements to the Microsoft 365 user context
  • Semantic labels that improve retrieval and Copilot understanding
  • Use a Microsoft or partner prebuilt connector when it meets security, coverage, permission, lifecycle, and support requirements.
  • Build a federated MCP connector when real-time data, external-system authority, and controlled actions are the priorities.
