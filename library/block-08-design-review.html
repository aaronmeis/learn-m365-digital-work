Block 8: Design review and teach-back

Block 8 of the Microsoft's Digital-Work Platform deep dive - design review and teach-back.

Time: 30 minutes · Goal: Defend the three views and the integration map to a skeptical architect

Focus from mind map

  • Use synced/indexed content when discovery, search quality, and repeated knowledge retrieval matter more than instantaneous freshness.
  • Use federated/MCP access when data is highly dynamic, sensitive, too large to duplicate, or must remain in its authoritative source.
  • Use API/workflow actions when the integration changes state.
